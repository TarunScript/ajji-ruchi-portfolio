Honouring a Father's Dream. Building a Brand for the Next Generation.

Every great journey begins with a dream. Girish Bannishetty’s began with his father's profound wish to see their family name stand tall in the world of authentic food. He envisioned Ajji Ruchi as a tribute to the warmth of grandmother's kitchen — honest ingredients, unforgettable aroma, and the comfort of home.

Taking that dream forward, Girish established M.G. Bannishetty Foods & Beverages. Under his leadership, the brand has scaled from family traditions into a 30+ SKU packaged food enterprise that preserves authentic regional recipes without cutting corners.
